the fine print

Commission Info

Everything worth knowing before you commission a piece.

Payment

  • All commissions must be paid upfront.
  • All prices are listed in USD.
  • Pay directly through this site or my Etsy shop. I also accept PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, and Apple Pay directly.
  • Open to alternative arrangements like art trades or gift cards. Just message me first.

Terms

  • Please give credit for all art to me, MissTy.
  • I can draw characters, real people, and pretty much any request, but I don't draw monsters.
  • Prices may change for excessive detail or repeated edits.
  • I reserve the right to decline any commission, whether it's outside my skill level, something I'm not comfortable with, or for any other reason.

Questions about any of this? Reach out before you order and I'm happy to talk it through.
